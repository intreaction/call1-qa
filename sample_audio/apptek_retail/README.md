`manifest.json` contains Call1's reformatted annotations for 30 human role-played retail scenarios. The adapted annotations retain CC-BY-SA 4.0. Full recordings and raw metadata are omitted. These are evaluation fixtures, not real customer records or a cleared model-training corpus.

Tests use the transcript annotations to check segmentation and masking. The separately distributed short Process demo excerpt identifies its exact edit and attribution in `call1/process/static/demo/NOTICE.md`.
